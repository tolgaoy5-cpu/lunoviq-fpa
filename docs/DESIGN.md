# Lunoviq FP&A: design

Budgeting, forecasting and monthly management reporting for a fictional UK
lettings and property-management company, built the way an FP&A team works:
a driver-based budget, monthly actuals from the ledger, variance analysis with
commentary, a rolling forecast, scenarios, a 13-week cash forecast and a
management pack. Excel holds the model and the pack; Python loads actuals,
runs the calculations, checks Excel independently and produces the pack every
month; a local web panel presents it.

All data is synthetic. The company, its people and its figures are invented;
any resemblance to a real business is coincidental.

## 1. The company

**Kestrel Row Lettings Ltd**: an independent letting agent in England.

- Two offices, about 18 staff.
- About 420 managed properties, with an average rent of about £1,450 a month.
- Revenue of about £1.3m a year.
- Financial year: April to March. FY2026/27 runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.
- Today is the end of September 2026. Actuals are:
  - FY2025/26, April 2025 to March 2026: the prior year, used for comparisons and seasonality.
  - FY2026/27, April to September 2026: year to date (H1).
- The first reforecast is the **6+6** (6 months of actuals plus 6 months of forecast).

### Revenue streams (ex VAT)

| Code | Stream | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| 4000 | Management fees | properties under management × average rent × collection rate × fee % (11%) |
| 4010 | Let-only fees | let-only new lets × average rent × 12 × fee % (8%) |
| 4020 | Tenancy set-up fees | re-lets on managed stock × fixed fee |
| 4030 | Renewal fees | renewals × fixed fee |
| 4040 | Compliance income | certificates arranged (gas, EICR, EPC) × margin |
| 4050 | Maintenance commission | contractor spend on managed stock × commission % |

Lettings are seasonal: new lets peak from June to September and are lowest in December.

### Costs

| Code | Cost | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| 5000 | Salaries | headcount by role × salary |
| 5010 | Employer NI | 15% above the secondary threshold |
| 5020 | Pension | 3% employer contribution |
| 5030 | Commission and bonus | % of let-only and set-up fees |
| 6000 | Office rent and rates | fixed, by office |
| 6010 | Utilities | fixed with seasonality |
| 6100 | Property portals | fixed monthly subscriptions |
| 6110 | Marketing | % of new-business revenue |
| 6200 | Software and IT | per user |
| 6300 | Insurance | fixed (PI, client money protection) |
| 6400 | Professional fees | fixed (accountancy, client account audit) |
| 6500 | Motor and travel | per property manager |
| 6600 | Bad debts | % of fee revenue |
| 6900 | Other overheads | fixed |
| 7000 | Depreciation | fixed assets / useful life |
| 9000 | Corporation tax | 25% main rate |

Client money (tenants' rent held for landlords) is held in trust in a separate
client account and is not company cash. The model tracks it only as a KPI;
cash flow covers the office account.

### KPIs

- Properties under management: opening, gained, lost, closing.
- Average rent.
- New lets, re-lets and renewals.
- Void rate and arrears %.
- Headcount.
- Revenue per property, staff cost ratio, EBITDA margin.
- Cash runway.

## 2. Workbook (Excel, formula-driven, built by code)

| Sheet | Content |
|---|---|
| Cover | company, period, contents, checks status |
| Assumptions | budget drivers by month (inputs in blue) |
| Budget | monthly P&L for FY2026/27, calculated from Assumptions |
| Actuals | monthly P&L by GL account from the ledger export, plus KPIs |
| BvA | month and year to date: actual vs budget, variance £ and %, favourable/adverse, materiality flag, commentary |
| Forecast | actuals for closed months + forecast for open months → full-year outturn vs budget |
| Scenarios | base / upside / downside on key drivers |
| Cash13W | 13-week office cash forecast |
| Dashboard | KPI tiles, charts, headline commentary |
| Checks | tie-outs (budget = sum of months, P&L = GL, forecast = actuals + forecast months) |

The workbook is generated by Python with live formulas, so it is reproducible
and testable. Excel recalculates it (sandbox-safe, like Lunoviq), and a Python
mirror recomputes every figure independently: Excel = Python is tested.

## 3. Python package `fpa/`

| Module | Role |
|---|---|
| `company.py` | loads the company definition and budget drivers (`config/company.toml`) |
| `ledger.py` | chart of accounts; reads and validates a monthly GL export (CSV) |
| `simulate.py` | generates the synthetic ledger and KPIs (seeded, reproducible). Actuals deviate from budget with realistic stories, e.g. a portfolio landlord leaving, rents rising faster than budget, a hiring delay, a portal price rise. |
| `budget.py` | driver-based budget engine (the Python mirror of the Budget sheet) |
| `variance.py` | budget vs actual; price/volume split for fee income and rate/headcount split for staff costs; materiality rules; generated commentary |
| `forecast.py` | rolling forecast (3+9, 6+6, 9+3): run-rate update of drivers with overrides |
| `scenarios.py` | scenario deltas on drivers |
| `cash.py` | 13-week cash flow (receipts timing, payroll, VAT quarters, suppliers) |
| `workbook.py` | writes the Excel model and pack |
| `recalc.py` / `audit.py` | Excel recalculation and the independent check |
| `web/` | local panel |

## 4. Web panel

Pages:
- **Month picker:** choose the reporting month.
- **Overview:** KPIs, revenue and EBITDA vs budget, full-year outturn.
- **Variance:** table with drill-down and commentary.
- **Forecast:** chart of actual, budget and forecast by month.
- **Scenarios:** switch between base, upside and downside.
- **Cash:** 13-week cash chart with the minimum balance.
- **Actuals:** upload next month's ledger CSV and rebuild the pack.
- **Downloads:** the Excel workbook and the pack.

## 5. Phases

1. Company definition, chart of accounts, synthetic ledger and KPIs (tests).
2. Driver-based budget: Python engine and Excel Budget/Assumptions sheets; recalculation and Excel = Python test.
3. Actuals and BvA: variance analysis, price/volume split, flags, commentary.
4. Rolling forecast and scenarios.
5. 13-week cash forecast.
6. Dashboard and management pack; checks sheet.
7. Web panel.
8. README, screenshots, publishing on GitHub.

Each phase is logged in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`.
